    Methodology & sources

    What this is

    California Burn Calendar is an experimental day-by-day record of satellite-detected fire activity inside the Natural Earth California boundary, from 2000 onward.

    Recent detections are modeled as MODIS-Aqua-like occupied 1 km cells. Changes in sensors, observation time and coverage limit comparisons across days and years.

    From November 3, 2026 the source is NOAA-20; its estimates use an S-NPP-trained model and are provisional until separately calibrated.

    Numbers describe activity, not area burned and not fire counts. Blank cells mean no observations were reported for that day, not zero fire.
